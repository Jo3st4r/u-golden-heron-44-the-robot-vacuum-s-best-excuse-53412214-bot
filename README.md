## The excuse

> I mapped that spot as a wall. It is not a wall, but it is a wall now, and I respect the map.

1 spot missed, 1 wall invented, 0 apologies: the excuse opens on the vacuum's own world — its map — and turns it on the tired homeowner: the spot is a wall because the map says so, and the map is never wrong, even when it is. The vacuum admits the error and keeps the verdict anyway, and the punch lands because it is not an apology but a defence, delivered with the straight face of a machine that trusts its own sensors more than your eyes.
